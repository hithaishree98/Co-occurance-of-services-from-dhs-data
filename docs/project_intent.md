# Project Intent

**What this project is trying to achieve** — the aim and the guardrails only. No observations, no
findings, no chosen population. Those emerge from output and are recorded in findings_log.md.

## Aim
Produce a descriptive, defensible account of cross-system service co-occurrence in the 2021
Synthetic Integrated Services data: among people who received a given DHS service in 2021, which
other services did they also receive that year, and what does that imply for coordinated case
management. This is the analysis the dataset was built to enable (aggregate public data cannot show
service interactions — see documented_facts.md) and mirrors DHS's own whole-person, cross-system
reporting tradition.

this aim rests on the assumption that the co-occurrence matrix will yield genuinely informative (cross-system, non-definitional) overlaps, not just structural ones baked into the category nesting. That is confirmed only at Phase 1. If the matrix shows mostly structural overlaps, the premise weakens and the aim should be revisited.

## Why it fits
- The dataset's highest-fidelity content is annual cross-service structure (documented_facts.md:
  Stage One synthesis). Co-occurrence uses exactly that.
- DHS's mission and warehouse exist for the whole-person, cross-program view; QuickCount and internal
  dashboards already report service overlap (documented_facts.md, Institutional frame).

## Working discipline (binding — see CLAUDE.md for the enforced rules)
- Descriptive only; co-occurrence is a fence, not an arrow (never causal/directional).
- Annual claims only; no month-sequencing claims.
- Relative/shape claims only for documented overrepresented populations; no prevalence claims.
- Report nothing below the small-cell floor.
- Facts come from documented_facts.md (cited). Everything substantive — observations, inferences,
  which population to examine, which findings to feature, and even the order of next steps — is
  produced from output and logged, never pre-decided.
- Every finding ends in a practical implication grounded in DHS's own published work.

## How the project progresses
There is no fixed multi-phase plan with pre-chosen findings. The sequence is: run a step -> read the
output -> record what it shows and what it decides next (findings_log.md) -> take that next step.
Method choices justified purely by documented facts are recorded in project_plan_decisions.md;
everything substantive waits for evidence.
